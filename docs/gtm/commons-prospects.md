# Commons prospects: who might run a Commons on Cahootz

Researched 2026-10-23. This is a list to work through, not a record of outreach. Nobody has been contacted, no forms were filled in, and no one was signed up for anything.

## How to read this

- **What Cahootz offers them:** a member-owned Commons with its own shared coin, member votes on proposals, Sage (the AI organizer that connects people who can help each other), family Commons, and local stores. The iPhone app is live. Android is on the way.
- **Fit score:** 3 = strong (members already vote or pool resources, a store or buy-local mission, steady communication). 2 = plausible. 1 = long shot or indirect (a directory, a developer, a group we couldn't fully verify).
- **Active signs** are dated where we could date them. "Unverified" means we couldn't confirm it on the group's own site or credible news. Check before you reach out. Member counts come from the group's site or news and can be out of date.
- **Contact route** is the group's own contact page, or a general inbox the group prints on its own site. We left out any email that belongs to a named person. Where a group publishes no route, it says so. Don't dig for one.
- A few groups are a different kind of lead than a member group: a federation, network, or developer that could reach many Commons at once. Those are marked "(partner)".
- Rows are grouped by segment. 53 groups across 7 segments.

## Cautions before you send anything

- **Money language.** Never call the coin an investment or promise returns. For lending and credit groups (Arise, Mission Asset Fund, the ROSCA network), avoid anything that sounds like banking or savings. Say what the coin is and who sets its rules.
- **Check facts the same week.** Numbers and dates here were gathered on 2026-10-23 from public pages and news. Several are from search summaries or old articles.
- **Gaps.** Housing co-ops and Latino-led food co-ops didn't turn up with verifiable 2026 activity. Hometown and diaspora groups were thin. Neighbors Helping Neighbors (Twin Cities) looked like a strong fit, but its newest dated post was Jan 2026, so we left it out.
- **Leads not verified, left off the list:** Berkeley's Parker Street housing co-op, Austin's Boomers senior co-op, and UHAB's incubator.
